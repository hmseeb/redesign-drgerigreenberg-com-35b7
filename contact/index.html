Before your first visit

What to expect

Your first appointment starts with an assessment. Using Traditional Chinese Medicine diagnostics alongside a structural evaluation, Dr. Geri works out where the imbalance actually lies — then chooses which of the five methods will help you most.

  • Wear comfortable clothing you can move in
  • Bring a list of your symptoms and when they’re worst during the day
  • Note any medications or supplements you’re currently taking
  • Allow time to relax afterwards — treatment is deeply calming

Not sure you’re ready? Call for a free consultation first. There’s no obligation to book treatment.
